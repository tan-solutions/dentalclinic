Top 5 Signs You Might Need a Root Canal

Tooth pain isn’t something to ignore — it can be a warning sign that your tooth is infected or badly damaged. A **root canal** can save the tooth, ease your pain, and restore normal function. Here are five clear signs you might need one.

1. Persistent Tooth Pain

If your tooth hurts constantly, especially when you chew or touch it, the inner nerve might be infected. A root canal removes the infection and saves the tooth.

2. Sensitivity to Hot or Cold

Does your tooth ache after drinking tea or cold water? Lingering sensitivity is often a sign of nerve damage that needs attention.

3. Swollen or Tender Gums

If your gums near a tooth feel sore or swollen, infection may have spread to the root. Timely treatment can prevent further damage.

4. Tooth Discoloration

A tooth turning gray or dark could mean the nerve inside is dying. Root canal therapy helps restore both its health and natural look.

5. Pimple-like Bump on Gums

A small bump or boil near your tooth might indicate an abscess. This needs quick dental care to stop the infection from spreading.

Ignoring these symptoms can lead to serious complications or tooth loss. Early diagnosis makes treatment simple and successful.
